How EDUSYNC Works

Get your campus onboarded and running under a unified administrative dashboard in four simple steps.

01

Register Institution

Admins fill out the registration form, provide license verification documents, and select their preferred custom subdomain.

02

Instant Provisioning

Once reviewed and approved by the super admin, a branded tenant portal is immediately deployed under `.edusync.com`.

03

Setup Academic Structure

Admins import departments, specializations, and classes. Faculty rosters and student profiles are uploaded in bulk via Excel spreadsheets.

04

Start Synchronized Tracking

Teachers mark student attendance and post assignments. Department HODs configure timetables and track student internal score reports.
